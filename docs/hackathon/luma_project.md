About Event
​Location: Grab @One North, 3 Media Close, Singapore, 138498
https://app.grab.com/s/JVr2uqAj
​Be among the first to build with unreleased GrabMaps APIs. This exclusive beta hackathon welcomes developers of all skill levels to rapidly prototype, stress-test, and shape the future of GrabMaps before official launch.

​You'll get elevated API credits throughout the day, hands-on guidance from Grab engineers, and ~7 hours of hacking time to bring your ideas to life.

All participants will also get free access to ChatGPT Pro to build with Codex thanks to OpenAI!

​Choose your track:

​🚗 Intelligent Mobility — Build solutions that improve how people, goods, and services move through cities. Think dynamic routing for delivery fleets, ETA comparisons across transport modes, or route planners that adapt to real-time incidents.

​🗺️ Discover the City — Maps are discovery engines. Build tools that help people uncover places, experiences, and communities — hyperlocal recommendations, curated map experiences, or social maps where users share favorite spots.

​📍 Smarter Places — Places have identity, activity, and context. Build tools that help businesses and users better understand them — richer POI pages, live trending location maps, or platforms that visualize activity around places.

​Schedule

​8:30 AM — Doors open

​9:15 AM — Grab engineering workshop

​9:45 AM–5:00 PM — Hacking time

​5:00 PM — Submissions due

​6:30 PM — Finals presentations

​~7:30 PM — Dinner

​Lunch, snacks, and dinner provided.

​Prizes Over $6,000 in total prizes across categories including Best Overall Project, Best Use of GrabMaps APIs, and Best Bug Hunter. All participants receive GrabMaps API credits and early access to the official platform at launch. Full prize details coming soon.

​This event is produced by 65labs.org, Singapore's most active AI builder collective, in partnership with the Grab team.

Agrim is an OpenAI Codex Ambassador, and at this event, all attendees will be provided ChatGPT Pro to build with Codex. These credits are provided by the Codex Ambassador program offered by OpenAI and help builders leverage Codex in their projects with ease. Thank you OpenAI!
